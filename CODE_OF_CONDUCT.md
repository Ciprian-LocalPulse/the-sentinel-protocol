# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

This project deals with sensitive domains — business communication,
biometric data, negotiation. Contributors and users are expected to
engage in good faith, with respect for the people whose data this system
processes and the people who maintain it.

## Expected Behavior

- Constructive, specific feedback in Issues and PRs.
- Respect for the ethical boundaries documented in
  [`docs/philosophy.md`](docs/philosophy.md) — proposals to relax them are
  welcome as discussion, not as unilateral changes.
- No harassment, discriminatory language, or personal attacks in any
  project space.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Using this project's issue tracker or discussions to solicit or share
  approaches for deceiving, surveilling, or manipulating third parties
  without their consent.
- Harassment of any kind, public or private.

## Enforcement

Reports of unacceptable behavior can be made via GitHub's reporting tools
or by contacting the maintainer directly. The maintainer will review and
respond to all reports, and may remove content, reject contributions, or
restrict participation for violations.

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions —
and to public conduct when representing the project.
